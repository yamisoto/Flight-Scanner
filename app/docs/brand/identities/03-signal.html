Our pick: Dana Air, 11:30. ₦81,000 more than the cheapest seat. The cheapest one rates 5.2 for reliability. This one rates 8.1.

Public Sans was built for government services: neutral, highly legible and made for forms and data. It lets the bold headings carry the personality while fare rules stay easy to scan.

Visual references

Distinct feature: the Departure Board

Search results are a live departure board. Times and fares flip into place letter by letter, and every flight carries an airport-style status: RELIABLE, MIXED or OFTEN LATE. The Skyfare Pick is the row lit in yellow.

The flip animation turns a wait into a moment, makes screenshots instantly recognisable on social, and becomes the app's signature home-screen widget in Phase 2.
