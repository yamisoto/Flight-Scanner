Distinct feature: Route Adire

Every route gets its own adire-style pattern, generated from its airport codes. Lagos to Abuja always looks the same, so people recognise their regular trip at a glance on trip cards, share images, app tiles and WhatsApp previews.

It's a brand system no foreign competitor can copy convincingly, and it scales to Africa in Phase 3: each new country brings its own textile tradition.
