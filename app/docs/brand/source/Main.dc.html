Distinct feature: the Reliability Dial

The logo mark becomes the product's signature UI. Every flight's reliability score is shown as a small dial, the Skyfare Pick wears a full-blue dial, and the loading state is the needle sweeping from 0 to 10.

One shape tells people what the brand stands for every time they see a result. It translates directly to the app's icon, widgets and push notifications in Phase 2.
